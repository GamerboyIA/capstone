Hello World #1

*About me / us*

Earth is becoming more damaged and uninhabitable every year. It's air is slowly getting more toxic, the water more polluted, the beautiful natural landscapes are being shaved bare and destroyed. Climate change is a real and present danger and humanity is the primary contributed to this threat.

While it would unfair to blame every individual person as equally responsible for the damage plaguing our planet it is fair to say that we all need to do what we can before its too late.

hello

The Earth is massive, full of unique landscapes and ecosystems hundreds of thousands of miles apart from each other. Yet its becoming increasingly difficult to find a place that hasn't been touched by humans. From small bits of trash left behind by travelers or trails carved into nature, to entire forests torn down and left bare or replaced by giant concrete buildings, we as a species have dramatically and irreversibly changed both for the better and definitely for the worse.

While most of the human population can claim to have little to no influence in climate change the human race has without a doubt left its mark.

There is very little 99% of people can do to have a significant effect on their own, but if everyone did what little they could; throw away their trash, recycle, walk instead of drive from time to time anything at all. But add them together and those little insignificant acts suddenly become very large and impactful its probably too late for these little actions even at this scale to make a huge difference but it could be the action needed to spark real change innovation and improvement. Because in the end if we do nothing then we are accepting our fate of dying a slow trash filled death alongside our planet
